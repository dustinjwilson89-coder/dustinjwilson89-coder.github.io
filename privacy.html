Winslow privacy policy

Winslow is a private, single-user app used only by its owner. It accesses only the owner's own Google account.

Google data

With the owner's permission, Winslow reads the owner's Google Calendar events (and, in later versions, creates events on calendars the owner chooses). It requests only the calendar.events scope.

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. Calendar data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than answering the owner's own requests.

To answer a request, relevant event details (such as titles and times) are sent to the AI model provider that generates Winslow's replies, and the reply is delivered to the owner through Telegram.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local token file.
